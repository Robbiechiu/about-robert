About

A decade of entrepreneurship across three countries

I bring the rigor of materials science — an engineer's obsession with data and precision — together with the proven ability to build cross-border ventures from zero to one, and transplant it all into modern greenhouse farming. The “Lettuce Prince” is reshaping what traditional agriculture can be, with an engineer's precision and a global outlook — using a scientific process to grow lettuce of impeccable quality.

  1. Academia × Manufacturing Materials engineering & mass production, UK

    Materials research at the University of Birmingham (golf-club-head alloys). Back in Taiwan, served as a design & development PM at Fusheng Precision, taking TaylorMade products from development to mass production — building a solid foundation in precision manufacturing and project management.

  2. Cross-border 0 → 1 Hands-on ventures across Southeast Asia and Greater China

    Guided a Dongguan tooling factory toward financial transparency and cloud management, and ran overseas real-estate projects in Cambodia — alongside cross-border ventures spanning energy fuel rods and carbon-fiber processing.

  3. Into Agriculture Robert Farm · Aquaponics

    Taiwan's first aquaponics farm to run commercially for five full years — building end-to-end strength from cultivation to operations to market.

  4. Now Fully controlled, automated greenhouse × AI

    The culmination of it all — using technology and data to redefine the rules of agriculture.
